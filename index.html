How It Works

Four steps from picking a flower to it landing on their desk.

  1. Pick Your Blooms

    Choose single stems, a bouquet or a bundle, and pick the colors for each flower.

  2. Write a Note

    Add a message for your recipient. You can send it anonymously if you like.

  3. Choose Delivery or Pickup

    Give us two class schedules for delivery, or book a pickup time at the booth.

  4. Pay with GCash

    Scan our QR, then enter your reference number so we can confirm your order.

    Questions

    Can’t find your answer here? Message Celadon on Facebook and a member will get back to you.

    Are the flowers real?

    No. Every bloom is handcrafted, so it keeps its shape and color long after Valentine’s week.

    Can I send it anonymously?

    Yes. Tick “Send anonymously” at checkout and we’ll leave your name off the card. We still need your details for the order.

    What if my recipient isn’t in class?

    That’s what the backup schedule is for. If we miss them at both, we’ll message you to arrange a pickup.

    How do I pay?

    Through GCash. Scan the QR at checkout, send the exact total, then type your reference number and upload a screenshot of the receipt.

    Can I change my order?

    Message us with your order code before the order window closes and we’ll update it.
